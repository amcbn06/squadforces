How to import
  1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: Send my CSES progress
  2. Open cses.fi/problemset while logged in to CSES, and click that bookmark.
  3. A tab opens here with what was found. Check it, then press Import.

Repeat step 2 and 3 whenever you want your new solves counted. Your first import records what you had already solved, so it doesn't count towards the 30-day ranking; solves that appear in later imports count from the day you import them. To keep this honest the app compares with your public page on CSES: you can't have more newly solved tasks than new submissions there.
